Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what Wild Field Files Publisher (the "Tool") accesses and how that information is handled. The Tool is a private application used only by the operator of the Wild Field Files channel to publish the channel's own videos.

What the Tool accesses

  • The channel's own accounts only. The operator signs in with the official login of each platform (Google for YouTube, Meta for Instagram, TikTok Login Kit for TikTok) and grants the Tool permission to upload videos and read basic account information, such as the account name, to confirm the right account is connected.
  • No other users. The Tool does not access, collect or store information about viewers, followers or any other user.

How information is stored

The access tokens issued by the platforms are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a file readable only by the operator. They are used solely to upload the channel's videos and are never sold, shared or sent to anyone other than the platform that issued them.

What the Tool sends to the platforms

The video files, titles, descriptions, captions and scheduling times of the channel's own posts, sent through each platform's official API.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the account settings of each platform: Google Account → Security → Third-party apps; Meta → Business integrations; TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Revoking access stops the Tool from posting.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
